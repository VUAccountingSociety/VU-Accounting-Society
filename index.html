Membership is open to everyone

All accounting majors are automatically members of the VU Accounting Society — no sign-up required. We also welcome any business major interested in learning about public accounting, networking with firms, and building their career. Come to any event and get involved!

Scholarships & programs

Public accounting firms recruit early. The sooner you get on their radar, the better your chances going into junior-year internship recruiting. Programs are organized by class year — deadlines move fast, so start in the fall semester.

Embark Scholars Program
8-week paid internship rotating through Audit, Tax, and Advisory. Multi-year program with a 95%+ full-time offer rate — one of the strongest freshman pipelines in public accounting.

Rise Leadership Conference
1–2 day experience at KPMG Lakehouse in Orlando. Networking with professionals and a potential internship interview pathway for first-gen, veteran, LGBTQ+, and students of color.

Firm Relations Officer

Help us connect students with firms

The Firm Relations Officer builds and maintains relationships between the VU Accounting Society and public accounting firms — coordinating events, managing firm communications, and helping members land opportunities.

Why apply?

Direct firm access

Build real relationships with recruiters and professionals at Big 4 and regional firms before internship season.

Resume differentiator

Leadership in a professional organization is one of the most valued signals for public accounting recruiting.

Impact your peers

Help fellow students land internships and connect with the firms that will shape their careers.

Grow as a leader

Develop project management, communication, and relationship-building skills that translate directly to public accounting.

Application process
1

Submit your application

Fill out the form below or email your resume and a brief statement of interest to the society. Tell us why you're interested in firm relations and what you'd bring to the role.

2

Interview with the officer team

Selected applicants will be invited for a brief conversation with society leadership. Expect questions about your interest in public accounting, your ideas for firm engagement, and your availability.

3

Decision & onboarding

All applicants will receive a response. The selected officer will be onboarded before the Fall Kickoff and introduced to the team and members.

Application form

Takes about 5 minutes. All accounting and business majors are welcome to apply.

Prefer to apply by email?

Send your resume and a short note about your interest to accountingsociety@vanguard.edu with the subject line "Firm Relations Application."
